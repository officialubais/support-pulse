# My agent: support-pulse
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps e-commerce customers track orders, check inventory, process returns, and escalate support tickets with a catalog of items and orders.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Customer order history, active support sessions, return preferences
- Tools: lookup_order, check_inventory, calculate_refund, create_support_ticket
- Catalog/UI: Orders (ORD-1001..ORD-1004) and Products (SKU-HEADPHONES, SKU-KEYBOARD, etc.)
- Image gen: Product visuals & replacement item previews
- Sandbox: n/a

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
